# Privacy Policy for Fluent Fold

**Last updated:** July 23, 2026

## Overview

Fluent Fold is a desktop file organization tool that helps you sort and manage files on your Windows device. This privacy policy explains what data Fluent Fold collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

Fluent Fold **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**. The app operates entirely locally on your device.

- **File metadata**: Fluent Fold reads file names, extensions, sizes, and dates solely to organize them according to your chosen rules. This data is processed in memory and is never sent to any external server.
- **App settings**: Your preferences (theme, organization mode, window position, etc.) are stored locally on your device using the Windows `ApplicationData` API.
- **Crash logs**: If the app encounters an error, anonymized crash information may be written to a local log file at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FluentFold\crash.log`. This log stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

### What We Do NOT Collect

- No personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No browsing history or internet activity
- No location data
- No user accounts or credentials
- No network requests — Fluent Fold does not make any outbound connections

## Data Storage

All data processed by Fluent Fold remains on your local machine. The app does not use cloud storage, remote servers, or third-party services.

## Third-Party Services

Fluent Fold does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.
